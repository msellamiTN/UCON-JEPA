## 5. Interpretation and limitations

The result establishes only that the listed invariants hold for the reachable states of this finite abstract model under the configured constants and transition relation.

It does not establish:
- correctness of the production policy evaluator or reference monitor;
- complete mediation of every real tool/API route;
- linearizability of any concrete external service;
- correctness of a specific credential/delegation implementation;
- safety of mutable resource updates under real concurrency;
- asynchronous job cancellation or completion safety;
- correctness of a write-ahead log or crash-recovery implementation;
- liveness, bounded latency, availability, or fairness;
- correctness for unbounded agents, capabilities, resources, or invocations;
- refinement of a production implementation to this model.

In particular, revocation and policy freshness pass because the abstract Accept transition atomically checks those predicates at the effect boundary. A real adapter must implement an equivalent protocol. If it cannot, the model must be changed to represent the actual race and the guarantee must be narrowed.

The negative controls are important: they demonstrate that the selected invariants detect the four deliberately introduced classes of defect. They do not demonstrate that the current abstraction covers every relevant defect.
