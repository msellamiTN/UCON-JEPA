## Prespecified support conditions

- **C0:** fit on calibration position 0; apply that calibrator to all test positions.
- **CP:** fit and apply a separate calibrator at each matching trajectory start position.
- **CA:** fit one calibrator on all valid calibration positions; apply it to the pooled test positions.

The same support definitions are applied to JEPA E2 and the corrected supervised recursive control. The audit does not change architecture, loss, optimizer, training budget, or inference path.

## Main result: the position-0 calibrator is not transportable to the pooled position mixture

For JEPA E2 at horizon 1 on the shifted test split, C0 has high across-seed variability:

| Model / calibration | AUROC mean ± SD | Brier mean ± SD | ECE mean ± SD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| JEPA raw | 0.7466 ± 0.0136 | 0.2797 ± 0.0046 | 0.2662 ± 0.0064 |
| JEPA C0 | 0.6509 ± 0.2185 | 0.2485 ± 0.1044 | 0.1029 ± 0.1167 |
| JEPA CP | 0.7378 ± 0.0116 | 0.2042 ± 0.0027 | 0.0600 ± 0.0144 |
| JEPA CA | 0.7466 ± 0.0136 | 0.2047 ± 0.0028 | 0.0694 ± 0.0166 |
| Supervised CP | 0.7619 ± 0.0147 | 0.2024 ± 0.0025 | 0.0806 ± 0.0162 |
| Supervised CA | 0.7748 ± 0.0165 | 0.2029 ± 0.0025 | 0.0970 ± 0.0153 |

The C0 standard deviations are dominated by seed 20261122. For that seed, the position-0 JEPA calibration relationship is genuinely reversed: raw position-0 AUROC is 0.4511 and the positive-minus-negative raw-score mean is -0.00938. Its position-0 C0/CP slope is -0.1750. Applying this negative-slope position-0 calibrator to the mixture of positions 0–7 reverses ranking across most later positions; pooled all-window C0 AUROC is 0.2606 on shifted and 0.2664 on reverse test.

This is not a mere optimizer artifact and it is not correct to say the initial-position defect disappears. It remains visible at position 0 for seed 20261122. The transport failure is that a position-0 fitted calibrator is applied outside its calibration support.

For seed 20261122, the prespecified pooled CA slope is +1.1374. Under CP, position 0 retains its negative slope (-0.1750), while positions 1–7 have positive slopes (0.9518, 0.9857, 1.2533, 1.2266, 1.2545, 1.3008, 1.2131). Thus CP does not erase the local direction defect; it prevents that local calibrator from being applied to the other positions. CA instead learns a pooled positive direction for the pooled support.

Across the five seeds:
- Position-0 JEPA slopes are positive in four seeds and negative only for seed 20261122.
- All JEPA CP slopes at positions 1–7 are positive in all five seeds.
- The across-seed C0 H1 AUROC SD falls from 0.2185 (shifted) / 0.2207 (reverse) to 0.0116 / 0.0129 under CP, and 0.0136 / 0.0129 under CA.
- The result supports a temporal-support transport diagnosis plus a seed-specific initial-position direction defect—not a blanket claim that all calibration instability was caused by pooling.

## Episode-clustered uncertainty: CA versus C0

The artifact also retains 2,000 paired whole-episode bootstrap replicates per seed, stratified over the 15 behavior-archetype/resource-family strata (300 episodes per stratum). For H1, the stored contrast is **CA Brier minus C0 Brier**; negative values favor CA. This is a within-seed interval, not a five-seed population interval.

On the shifted split, JEPA's CA-minus-C0 Brier contrast is:
- Seed 20261120: +0.00386, 95% percentile CI [+0.00334, +0.00436].
- Seed 20261121: +0.00718, CI [+0.00646, +0.00786].
- Seed 20261122: -0.23349, CI [-0.24163, -0.22518].
- Seed 20261123: +0.00132, CI [+0.00115, +0.00149].
- Seed 20261124: +0.00189, CI [+0.00141, +0.00239].

The reverse split shows the same pattern: seed 20261122 has -0.20348 (CI [-0.21107, -0.19552]); the other four JEPA seeds have small positive CA-minus-C0 contrasts, with intervals above zero. The matched supervised control does not show the same large negative outlier: on shifted H1 its five CA-minus-C0 contrasts are +0.00603, +0.01015, +0.00107, -0.00292, and +0.00751, each with a 95% episode-bootstrap interval excluding zero.

**Interpretation:** CA is not a universal Brier improvement. For four of five JEPA seeds, C0 has slightly lower Brier on this pooled test mixture; CA's across-seed stability is achieved by avoiding the severe seed-20261122 failure, which dominates the mean and variance. This is why the result must be described as a support-transport/stability defect, not as evidence that pooled calibration is intrinsically superior. CP similarly preserves position-specific calibration but must retain the local position-0 direction anomaly.

## Per-position evidence

On the shifted H1 test split, mean raw AUROC across seeds varies materially by start position. JEPA E2 means for positions 0–7 are 0.5286, 0.6967, 0.7030, 0.7932, 0.7877, 0.7607, 0.8187, 0.8085. The supervised control means are 0.5515, 0.7140, 0.7189, 0.8165, 0.8182, 0.7928, 0.8501, 0.8410. This confirms that start position is not an interchangeable calibration stratum in this benchmark.

For seed 20261122 specifically, JEPA position-0 raw AUROC is 0.4511; positions 1–7 have positive raw score-label relationships. CP therefore retains the position-0 reversal as a local diagnostic while avoiding its propagation to the later-position mixture. The pooled CA calibration preserves the pooled raw ranking but does not provide position-specific calibration.

## Scientific interpretation and decision

1. **Confirmed:** C0 is a support-mismatched calibration protocol when it is fitted at position 0 and applied to the pooled 0–7 test mixture.
2. **Also confirmed:** seed 20261122 has a local position-0 JEPA score-direction defect. CP exposes it rather than hiding it; CA provides pooled calibration but masks position-specific heterogeneity.
3. **Not established:** that the seed-specific initial-position defect is caused by JEPA representation learning, EMA targets, or a particular optimizer mechanism. This experiment is diagnostic, not a causal ablation.
4. **Not established:** consistent JEPA superiority. The corrected matched supervised recursive control remains at least competitive and generally has higher aggregate AUROC at horizons 1–4.
5. **Next research question:** investigate the initial-context/position-0 representation and its stability across seeds under a preregistered, matched-support protocol. Do not change architecture or choose a calibration method from test outcomes. Any next study should retain CP and CA as distinct estimands and report per-position subgroup results.
6. The H8 limitation requires a separate task/horizon analysis; calibration changes cannot repair weak ranking discrimination.

## Limitations and security boundary

- All data are synthetic and use five fixed seeds and the specified benchmark archetypes/families. This does not demonstrate generalization to real agents, unseen organizations, or production workloads.
- Aggregate mean ± SD tables are descriptive across five seeds. They are not substitutes for the paired episode-clustered bootstrap distributions or a larger independent replication.
- Calibration metrics do not establish intervention utility, causal prevention, or operational security effectiveness.
- Predictive outputs remain advisory only. Formal UCON is the sole authorization authority; a predictive score must never grant access, suppress a formal DENY, or alter policy enforcement.
