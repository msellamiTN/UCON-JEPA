The preregistered recurrence threshold is at least 4/5 supported-negative JEPA cells in a row or column. JEPA-specific recurrence additionally requires at most 1/5 supported-negative supervised cells and at least 4/5 negative paired JEPA-minus-supervised raw-AUROC and calibration-slope contrasts.

**Model-training-seed columns:** training seed 20261122 had 4/5 supported-negative JEPA cells and 0/5 supported-negative supervised cells. All five paired raw-AUROC contrasts and all five calibration-slope contrasts in this column were negative. This meets the preregistered JEPA-specific training-seed recurrence rule. No other training-seed column met the threshold.

For the five datasets at training seed 20261122, JEPA shifted-test AUROC was approximately 0.4595, 0.4523, 0.4581, 0.4839, and 0.4608. Matched-supervised AUROC was approximately 0.5394, 0.5340, 0.5389, 0.5475, and 0.5428. Four JEPA cells were supported-negative; the fifth was weak/inconclusive because its AUROC CI touched 0.5. All five supervised cells were supported-positive.

In each of the four supported-negative JEPA cells, all four required bootstrap intervals were negative: calibration score-direction delta, calibration slope, shifted-test score-direction delta, and shifted-test AUROC. The fifth cell's AUROC CI was [0.4676, 0.5005], so the conservative weak/inconclusive classification is retained.

Paired bootstrap intervals in all five cells of this column favored supervised on raw AUROC and calibration slope. Calibrated Brier contrasts overlapped zero; this does **not** establish a calibrated-probability advantage. Across all 25 cells, mean JEPA-minus-supervised raw AUROC was -0.02777 and mean calibration-slope contrast was -0.12924. These averages are descriptive.

## 5. Interpretation and limits

**Supported:** the negative JEPA initial-position direction is not explained by a particular dataset seed. In this preregistered 5 × 5 synthetic benchmark, it recurs across four of five datasets for one combined JEPA model-training seed, while the matched supervised control does not show the same failure. Evidence points to training-stochasticity sensitivity in this JEPA setup.

**Not established:** initialization alone as the cause; general JEPA failure across arbitrary seeds or datasets; real-agent transfer; production security assurance; intervention utility; a calibrated Brier advantage; or a separately identified dataset × training interaction.

Raw AUROC is weak-to-moderate and often near chance outside the problematic column. This benchmark does not demonstrate a general JEPA-specific predictive advantage.
