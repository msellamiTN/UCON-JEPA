## 6. Seed-20261122 calibration failure

The JEPA H=1 Platt calibrator in seed 20261122 had a negative slope and inverted the test ranking (shifted AUROC 0.261, Brier 0.435; reverse AUROC 0.266, Brier 0.412). The matched supervised control did not exhibit this failure (shifted AUROC 0.793, Brier 0.198; reverse AUROC 0.762, Brier 0.201). This is a material model/calibration robustness failure, not an outlier to discard. It substantially affects pooled H=1 JEPA results and must be analyzed separately before any deployment claim.

## 7. Scientific verdict

**Established:**
1. The corrected supervised comparator has coherent recursive state semantics: predict next observable context, re-encode it with the action-head context encoder, then continue the rollout.
2. The matched five-seed benchmark and CI suites passed, with all datasets and results retained.
3. The supervised recursive control matches 91.1% of JEPA's trainable parameter count and uses the same data partitions, action-head class, optimizer budget and inference information boundary.
4. JEPA does not consistently outperform the supervised state-dynamics control. Supervised E2 is better at H=1/2 on pooled shifted metrics; H=4/8 are near ties with seed/subgroup reversals.

**Not established:**
1. A JEPA-specific representation or objective advantage.
2. Robustness across all archetypes, families, horizons or seeds.
3. Real-agent transfer, intervention utility, or production security guarantees.
