# G8.4.8.9 — Prior-art and implementation audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** targeted scoping audit; not a systematic literature review.  
**Purpose:** prevent novelty inflation and identify which source-paper components are reused, which assumptions need revalidation, and what is specific to UCON-JEPA.

## 1. Primary paper

Balestriero, Randall; LeCun, Yann; Varaku, Kerda. (2026). *Counterfactual World Models: JEPAs That Reason About "What If"*. arXiv:2611.24778, submitted 2026-11-18. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24778

The paper's central distinction is between:
- factual prediction: the conditional average outcome under an action;
- individual counterfactual prediction: the outcome this same event would have produced under an alternative action, keeping the realized disturbance fixed.

Its construction is residual reuse:
\[
\widehat z_{t+1}(a')=z_{t+1}+\mu(z_t,a')-\mu(z_t,a_t).
\]

The paper proves factual-law non-identification of individual counterfactual coupling, exactness under additive shared disturbances, a residual-risk identity controlled by variance of individual action effects, an interaction bound, and a short-time result under a smooth separate-channel expansion. It further derives a minimax lower bound for its stated model class and gives empirical positive/negative controls.

**Important limitations from the paper itself:**
- factual predictive accuracy does not establish same-disturbance counterfactual identification;
- action support, conditional exchangeability and state sufficiency are separate requirements;
- disturbances that multiply the command, contacts/clipping/saturation, and folded or insufficient representations can invalidate residual reuse;
- longer horizons accumulate interaction and require one-step corrections or additional persistence assumptions;
- the short-time guarantee is model-class-specific and cannot be transferred to arbitrary discrete policy systems.

## 2. Official implementation inspected

Repository: https://github.com/galilai-group/counterfactual-WM  
Default branch observed: main  
Repository license: MIT (repository metadata and LICENSE). Third-party notices separately identify assets with other licenses.

Files inspected:
- README.md — installation, reproduction stages, data/model workflow and quick-start API.
- cwm/counterfactual.py — residual_transport and readout_transport.
- cwm/model.py — frozen encoder, one-frame factual predictor, physical readout, counterfactual inference.
- cwm/predictor.py — history predictor and factual squared-loss training/splitting.
- cwm/evaluate.py and cwm/metrics.py — paired-population scoring, whole-parent bootstrap, interaction diagnostics and qualification gates.
- cwm/sims/collect.py — factual-only collection and replay-based rendering.
- pyproject.toml — dependencies and packaging.

The released helper implements the same arithmetic for a bank of alternatives:
\[
z_{\mathrm{cf}}=z_{\mathrm{observed}}+\mu_{\mathrm{alternatives}}-\mu_{\mathrm{factual}}.
\]
The model applies readout transport for physical coordinates. Its benchmark population has parent episodes, disturbance draws and multiple action branches; factual predictors are fitted from factual transitions, while paired simulator outcomes are retained for counterfactual evaluation. Its metrics include action-effect diagnostics, oracle-without-outcome and factual-copy references, interaction ratios, and whole-parent uncertainty intervals.

The implementation is not copied into UCON-JEPA. The new reference helper is a small independent NumPy implementation with explicit rejection of categorical values and non-finite/shape-invalid inputs. If any future source-code reuse occurs, retain MIT attribution and inspect all third-party notices before importing simulator assets or vendor code.

## 4. UCON-JEPA-specific research gap (candidate, not a novelty claim)

The candidate gap is not "apply CWM to security." It is to construct and validate a **typed, policy-aware counterfactual evaluation system** where:

1. continuous state readouts can use residual reuse only after regime-specific structural qualification;
2. categorical current authorization outcomes are recomputed by the formal UCON policy oracle, never obtained by latent arithmetic;
3. future discrete event probabilities are separately forecast and calibrated;
4. action alternatives are evaluated with paired same-disturbance simulator branches and exact branch lineage;
5. agent replanning is allowed to diverge under an alternative intervention, rather than incorrectly holding future actions fixed;
6. unsupported/invalid counterfactual queries are detected and abstained from;
7. individual counterfactual prediction is kept distinct from strategy-level randomized ITT intervention utility.

The potential contribution must be demonstrated against direct factual JEPA, supervised dynamics, residual transport, action-discriminative baselines where feasible, a structural UCON oracle for immediate verdicts, and a privileged paired-supervision ceiling. No contribution claim is defensible without held-out paired counterfactual ground truth and failure-regime analysis.

## 5. Literature search record and remaining review

Targeted search terms used on 2026-11-21 included:
- "Counterfactual World Models" and "same disturbance" JEPA;
- "counterfactual prediction" world models and factual continuation;
- action-discriminative JEPA/world models for MPC;
- counterfactual identifiability, structural causal models, rank preservation;
- agent runtime authorization and policy-conditioned trajectory counterfactuals.

This is a targeted scoping audit, not a PRISMA-style systematic review. Before manuscript submission, expand the review to:
- causal process mining and prescriptive process monitoring;
- counterfactual explanations for sequential decision systems;
- dynamic treatment regimes and sequential causal inference;
- policy-aware model-based planning and safe reinforcement learning;
- usage-control and ABAC/UCON policy evolution under autonomous agents;
- counterfactual evaluation of tool-using LLM/agent workflows;
- uncertainty-aware selective prediction and abstention for model-based control.

For each added work, record exact task, estimand, data pairing, disturbance assumptions, discrete/continuous outcome handling, authorization boundary, evaluation oracle, and whether individual counterfactuals or only population effects are identified.
