## 5. Scientific verdict

1. **CI/implementation gate: passed.** Full five-seed experiment and complete G8.4 regression suite succeeded; artifact and checksum retained.
2. **JEPA representation advantage: not demonstrated.** Joint JEPA is close to supervised context on rho=0.85 and worse on key pooled losses at rho=0.05.
3. **Post-hoc latent head: weak comparator outcome.** Joint JEPA substantially outperforms it, but that alone does not demonstrate JEPA's value over a non-JEPA supervised predictor.
4. **Heterogeneity remains material.** The retrying-adversary result and reverse-shift archetypes prevent a general robustness claim.
5. **Scope boundary:** these are synthetic, one-step action-prediction experiments. They do not validate recursive E1/E2 forecasting, future security violations, intervention utility, real-agent transfer, or authorization safety.
