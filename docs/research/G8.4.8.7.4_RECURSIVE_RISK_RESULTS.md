Interpretation:

These contrasts quantify the cost of replacing realized future actions with sampled predicted actions in this setup. They do **not** establish a JEPA advantage over another deployable model, and E1 is a privileged information condition rather than a fair deployable competitor.

## 5. Subgroup heterogeneity

Across 75 seed × archetype × family subgroup cells per horizon in the primary shifted condition, E2 Brier is worse than the train-prevalence baseline in:

- H=1: 22/75 cells.
- H=2: 6/75 cells.
- H=4: 8/75 cells.
- H=8: 37/75 cells.

At H=8, AUROC is undefined in 15/75 subgroup cells because a subgroup contains only one target class. The full per-archetype/family metrics are retained in the artifact. Pooled gains at H=2/H=4 therefore must not be interpreted as uniform subgroup robustness.

## 6. Scientific verdict

**Established by this experiment**

1. The corrected recursive benchmark executes reproducibly across five seeds.
2. The calibration instability found in the first run is addressed by a tested numerical correction.
3. Under the synthetic rho=0.85 shift, E2 contains pooled future-DENY predictive signal at H=2 and H=4 relative to a horizon-matched constant training-prevalence baseline.
4. E2 loses substantial predictive information relative to the privileged known-action diagnostic, with positive paired episode-bootstrap differences across all five seeds and all tested horizons.

**Not established**

1. JEPA-specific benefit: no matched supervised recursive state-transition predictor is included in this experiment.
2. Robustness across archetypes/families: subgroup reversals and H=8 single-class subgroups remain.
3. Strong H=1 or H=8 predictive utility.
4. Real-agent transfer, intervention utility, or production security guarantees.

## 7. Required next experiment

Before claiming a JEPA contribution, implement a deployable, matched supervised recursive comparator using the same initial context, past-only action information, action sampling budget, split boundaries, calibration data, horizons, seeds and evaluation metrics as E2. Add matched parameter/capacity and training-budget controls where feasible. Compare JEPA E2 against this baseline with paired whole-episode bootstrap and subgroup reporting.

Formal UCON remains the sole authorization authority. Predictive scores cannot authorize operations or override a formal DENY.
