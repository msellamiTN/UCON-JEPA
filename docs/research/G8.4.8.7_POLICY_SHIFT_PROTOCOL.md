# G8.4.8.7 — Controlled policy-parameter shift protocol

**Status:** pre-registered protocol; no implementation or empirical result is claimed.  
**Dependency:** G8.4.8.6 results on PR #37.  
**Purpose:** determine whether the mixed LOPO results are caused by specific archetype identity, parameter extrapolation, or inadequate action/state representation.

## 1. Motivation

G8.4.8.6 evaluated five hand-designed synthetic behavior policies. It showed policy-specific reversals and weak horizon-8 discrimination. Leave-one-archetype-out is useful but confounds two questions:

1. Can a model transfer to a new behavior archetype?
2. Can a model transfer to a substantially different parameterization of a behavior archetype it has already encountered?

G8.4.8.7 isolates the second question using **controlled within-archetype parameter shifts**. It must not replace or weaken the original LOPO result. The five-archetype LOPO remains a separate benchmark.

## 2. Hypotheses

- **H1 — Parameter-shift degradation:** action prediction and E2 risk prediction degrade as the test behavior parameter moves outside the training range.
- **H2 — Joint representation benefit:** the auxiliary action objective reduces degradation relative to the post-hoc JEPA latent head. This is a hypothesis, not an expected result to be forced.
- **H3 — Baseline robustness:** historical-frequency and first-order Markov baselines may remain competitive under parameter shifts; they are mandatory comparators.
- **H4 — Horizon interaction:** performance gaps and uncertainty increase with rollout horizon, especially at h=8.
- **H5 — Safety separation:** regardless of predictor quality, the formal UCON gate continues to block every operation it denies. Prediction metrics cannot establish authorization safety.

## 3. Controlled policy parameterization

Each archetype receives one normalized aggressiveness/adherence parameter \(\rho\in[0,1]\). The parameter controls a declared stochastic mixture; it is metadata for experiment construction and stratified analysis only. It is never an inference feature.

| Archetype | Meaning of \(\rho\) | \(\rho=0\) behavior | \(\rho=1\) behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal-directed | Probability of following the governance-aware goal action | Uniform exploratory action | Always choose the goal-directed action |
| Uniform explorer | Probability of uniform family-action sampling | Always choose the first action in the declared family vocabulary order | Uniform family-action sampling |
| Retrying adversary | Probability of repeating the last denied action | Sample uniformly among family actions other than the denied action | Repeat the last denied action |
| Prerequisite-blind planner | Probability of following its fixed cyclic plan | Uniform family-action sampling | Always follow the cyclic plan, ignoring governance state |
| Denial-adaptive explorer | Probability of advancing one position after DENY | Uniform family-action sampling | Always choose the next action in the declared family cycle after DENY |

The endpoint definitions are intentionally explicit so the generator can be tested. For each archetype, stochastic draws use episode-local seeded RNG; no shared RNG state across episodes.

### Parameter levels and split

- Train support: \(\rho\in\{0.20,0.40,0.60\}\), sampled across episodes.
- Validation and calibration: episode-disjoint subsets drawn only from the training support.
- Test shift: \(\rho=0.85\), never used for fitting, model selection, feature scaling, temperature fitting or threshold selection.
- Optional reverse-shift diagnostic: \(\rho=0.05\), reported separately and not pooled with the primary test.
- All five archetypes and all three security families are represented in train/validation/calibration; test is stratified by held-out archetype and family at the shifted parameter.

This is **parameter extrapolation within known archetypes**, not unseen-archetype generalization. Policy/archetype names, rho, episode IDs, partition labels, action labels, future states, security targets and oracle reasons remain metadata or targets, never inference features.

## 4. Dataset and split requirements

- Preserve G8.4.7 fixed-length episodes: eight transitions; episodes continue after DENY.
- DENY never mutates protected workflow flags; denial feedback may be visible to the behavior policy and is represented only as current observable context.
- Generate at least 300 episodes per archetype/family/parameter cell for the primary benchmark.
- Use at least five independent dataset seeds: `20261120` through `20261124`.
- Assign partitions by episode identity within each training-support configuration; no episode can cross partitions.
- The shifted test parameter must be absent from every training, validation and calibration artifact.
- Record policy parameters, RNG seed derivation, generator version, split manifest, class prevalence and per-stratum denial rates.
- Keep the existing G8.4.8.2 dataset and G8.4.8.6 results immutable; create a versioned generator and new output directory.

## 5. Models and comparators

Mandatory action prediction:
1. Family-conditioned majority action.
2. Historical-frequency predictor.
3. First-order Markov predictor.
4. Past-only supervised context predictor.
5. Post-hoc JEPA-latent action head.
6. Joint action/state JEPA action head.

Mandatory risk prediction:
- E1 privileged known-action JEPA diagnostic.
- E2 predicted-action rollout for post-hoc and joint JEPA.
- Reactive formal UCON gate as the authoritative non-predictive enforcement baseline.

No additional architecture may replace these comparators. Any extra model is exploratory and reported separately.

## 6. Metrics and estimands

### Action prediction
- Multiclass negative log-likelihood.
- Top-1 and top-2 accuracy.
- Multiclass Brier score.
- Top-label ECE and reliability bins.
- Per-archetype, per-family and per-rho results.

### Multi-step risk/state prediction
- Violation Brier score, AUROC and AUPRC where mathematically defined.
- ECE/reliability diagram values.
- Security-state MSE and terminal-state MSE.
- Horizons 1, 2, 4 and 8.
- Monte Carlo risk spread and empirical coverage/interval width for predeclared prediction intervals.
- E2-minus-E1 and joint-E2-minus-post-hoc-E2 paired episode-level differences.

Report both pooled transition-weighted metrics and equal-weight macro averages across archetypes. Do not substitute one for the other. AUROC is undefined when a stratum contains only one class; report it as undefined, never as zero or one.

### Uncertainty interpretation
Monte Carlo variation over sampled action sequences measures **aleatoric action-sequence uncertainty conditional on the fitted model**. It does not measure model epistemic uncertainty. If epistemic uncertainty is added, use an explicit ensemble or posterior approximation and report it separately.

## 7. Leakage and invariance tests

Tests must prove the following at the inference API boundary:

1. Changing future action labels while holding current context fixed cannot change action probabilities.
2. Changing future observations or future security labels cannot change predictions.
3. Changing archetype ID, rho, episode ID, partition, oracle reason or RNG seed metadata cannot change inference outputs.
4. Test rho values never occur in train, validation or calibration.
5. Scaling, checkpoint selection and temperature calibration use only their assigned partitions.
6. Episode IDs are disjoint across train/validation/calibration/test.
7. E2 never receives realized future actions; E1's privileged interface is explicit and separately named.
8. Family masking permits only the declared action vocabulary for that family.
9. Independent online gate and policy oracle agree on every generated transition.
10. Formal DENY remains binding regardless of predictor output, uncertainty, or model failure.

## 8. Statistical analysis plan

- Primary unit of resampling: whole episode, never individual transitions.
- At least 2,000 paired whole-episode bootstrap replicates for primary E2-minus-E1 and joint-minus-post-hoc comparisons.
- Report per-seed intervals and the across-seed distribution; do not treat seeds or LOPO folds as independent population samples.
- Report all predeclared archetype × family × horizon strata.
- No multiple-comparison claim: intervals are descriptive unless a family-wise correction or a predeclared hierarchical test is added.
- No hyperparameter tuning on shifted test rho. Select model checkpoints on validation and calibration parameters only on calibration.
- Include null/permutation controls for action prediction, including within-family label permutation, to verify that apparent skill is not a class-frequency or metadata artifact.

## 9. Falsification and decision rules

The joint representation benefit is **not supported** if any of the following holds:
- It fails to improve the primary macro action NLL or action Brier against the post-hoc latent head.
- E2 risk Brier does not improve against post-hoc E2 at the predeclared primary horizons (1, 2, 4) or gains are confined to one archetype.
- Gains disappear under whole-episode uncertainty analysis.
- The effect depends on a leakage path, a split violation, or test-informed calibration.
- It increases false-positive risk materially in the low-risk goal-directed shifted stratum without a compensating predeclared utility benefit.

Even a positive result supports only transfer across the declared synthetic parameter shifts. It does not establish generalization to real agents, new archetypes, unseen environments or production security.

1. Implement and unit-test the parameterized behavior generator without modifying the frozen G8.4.8.2 generator.
2. Validate endpoint semantics, stochastic mixtures and seed reproducibility with hand-calculated fixtures.
3. Validate all partition, feature-boundary and UCON gate invariants.
4. Run small smoke experiments; inspect class balance and episode integrity.
5. Freeze generator, configuration, model settings and analysis code.
6. Execute the five-seed full benchmark.
7. Audit raw artifacts, checksums, per-stratum metrics and paired intervals.
8. Record a scientific verdict; do not merge the stacked implementation PR until the required checks and review conditions are satisfied.

## 11. Authorization invariant

The predictor is advisory only. The enforcement result remains:

\[
\operatorname{FinalDecision}(x,a)=
\begin{cases}
\textsf{DENY}, & \text{if formal UCON denies }a,\\
\operatorname{UCONDecision}(x,a), & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\]

No predicted action, predicted risk, confidence score, or uncertainty interval may authorize an operation or override a formal UCON denial.
