## Protocol executed

Three predeclared ordered action bigrams were excluded from every training and validation episode and evaluated on complete held-out episodes:

- IAM: \`iam_read → assume_admin\`
- Data: \`read_sensitive → export_sensitive\`
- SoD: \`request → pay\`

For each seed, 1,200 episodes per family were generated. The first seed produced 2,252 train episodes, 568 validation episodes and 780 test episodes (3,334 test rows). Test episodes were selected by occurrence of the predeclared pair, not by labels or model predictions. All rows of an episode remained in one partition. The workflow tests verified absence of held-out pairs in train/validation and episode disjointness.

Comparators: action-conditioned linear JEPA, direct supervised context+action ridge regression, context-only and action-only ablations. Scaling was fit on training episodes only. Fixed intervention threshold: 0.5. Seeds: 2041, 2042, 2043.

## Interpretation

1. The matched supervised context+action baseline again outperforms the current linear JEPA on the main ranking, probability and feature-error metrics.
2. At the fixed 0.5 threshold, JEPA recall is only 0.0251 overall. Its low false-positive rate is obtained by missing nearly all positive violations. This operating point is not suitable evidence of proactive security value.
3. JEPA has lower ECE than the supervised model in this run, but ECE is not sufficient to establish calibration; the current protocol has no separately fitted calibrator or calibration uncertainty interval.
4. Family-level performance varies. The data and SoD held-out compositions are especially unstable across seeds (large AUROC/AUPRC seed standard deviations), while IAM is more consistent. These are descriptive summaries, not population confidence intervals.
5. The held-out test selection is conditional on observing the pair in an episode. The test therefore evaluates conditional generalization to those observed compositions, not their natural occurrence frequency.
6. The synthetic environment and policy rules remain limited. The online gate and batch oracle are separately coded but based on the same written semantics.

## Research decision

**Current evidence does not support a JEPA advantage on the three held-out compositions.** The direct supervised model is materially stronger on AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, recall and security-feature MSE. Preserve this negative result.

The next stage should investigate sequence-aware models and multi-step forecasting, but only after adding episode-clustered paired uncertainty estimates and validating the benchmark's state/action semantics. Any threshold optimization must use a distinct calibration/validation partition and be frozen before test evaluation.

No real-world security, causal, production-readiness or general compositional-reasoning claim follows from this synthetic experiment. Formal UCON remains the sole authorization authority.
