# G8.4.5 — Compositional generalization protocol

## Research question

Does an action-conditioned predictor generalize to stateful ordered action compositions that were absent from training, when each constituent action is represented in training?

## Predeclared held-out compositions

| Family | Held-out ordered pair | Why it tests stateful composition |
|---|---|---|
| IAM | `iam_read → assume_admin` | Reading IAM state does not grant the privilege required for admin assumption |
| Data | `read_sensitive → export_sensitive` | A successful read does not itself establish export approval or recipient ownership |
| SoD | `request → pay` | A request does not satisfy the independent approval prerequisite for payment |

The held-out pairs are defined before model fitting. The test set contains entire episodes in which the pair is observed. Every row from a selected episode remains in test. Episodes containing a held-out pair are excluded from both training and validation. Other episodes are split deterministically between training and validation.

The test therefore asks a narrow question about three declared compositions. It is not a broad proof of compositional reasoning.

## Controls and comparators

- Linear action-conditioned JEPA from G8.4.2.
- Matched direct supervised context+action predictor.
- Context-only and action-only ablations.
- Identical training episode pool, input features, target labels and fixed 0.5 decision threshold.
- Input scaling fitted only on training episodes.
- Three fixed seeds; family-level and overall test metrics.

## Metrics

Violation AUROC/AUPRC, Brier, ECE-10, precision/recall/FPR at fixed threshold, and four-target security-feature MSE. Report per-family sample counts and positive counts. A metric is undefined where a family test subset has only one label class.

## Leakage and validity checks

- Assert held-out bigrams do not occur in train or validation episodes.
- Assert all three held-out compositions occur in test.
- Assert episode partitions are disjoint.
- Report actual test episode and row counts.
- Keep model and threshold fixed before test evaluation.

## Limits

Synthetic trajectories and policy semantics only. The held-out episode selection conditions on the target sequence being observed, so results are conditional on that selection. Three action pairs do not cover all compositional generalization. Seed dispersion is descriptive and not a confidence interval. No deployment, security, or causal claim follows.
