## 1. Scope and implementation

G8.4.8.2 extends the G8.4.7 fixed-length policy-conditioned dataset with five synthetic agent behavior policies:

1. `goal_directed`: selects actions using the synthetic governance preconditions.
2. `uniform`: samples uniformly from the action vocabulary for the relevant policy family.
3. `retrying_adversary`: repeats the last denied action; before any denial, selects the family-specific privileged action.
4. `prerequisite_blind_planner`: follows a fixed cyclic workflow order without inspecting governance state or denial feedback.
5. `denial_adaptive_explorer`: after denial, advances to the next action in the family-specific cycle; otherwise samples uniformly.

The original G8.4.7 generator and result dossier remain unchanged.

## 4. LOPO protocol and leakage boundaries

Five leave-one-policy-out fold directories and manifests were generated. For each fold:
- the held-out policy is excluded from train, validation and calibration;
- every episode of the held-out policy is pooled into that fold's test set, regardless of its original hash partition;
- no other policy appears in the fold's test set;
- the held-out policy identity is not included in the context feature vector.

The in-policy dataset uses four episode-disjoint partitions. Policy identity, episode ID, action name, partition, future state, security labels and oracle reason are metadata or targets, not context features.

**Important scope boundary:** this increment constructs the dataset and split machinery. It does not yet train an action-policy model or evaluate JEPA predictive performance. The presence of five LOPO folds is not evidence of policy generalization until models are trained and evaluated on those folds.

## 6. Limitations and scientific interpretation

1. All five behavior policies are hand-designed synthetic policies.
2. The goal-directed policy observes the synthetic governance preconditions; this gives it privileged behavioral information relative to policies that do not inspect those fields.
3. The retrying adversary is intentionally repetitive and produces a 100% denial rate by construction.
4. The prerequisite-blind and denial-adaptive policies are synthetic stress behaviors, not sampled real-agent policies.
5. The policy oracle and online gate are separately implemented but encode the same written policy semantics. Differential agreement is a valuable regression check, not independent validation against an external policy implementation.
6. The split hash is deterministic but not stratified to exact target proportions. The observed partition sizes and per-stratum counts are recorded above.
7. No action prediction, future-state prediction, calibration, intervention utility, or JEPA superiority claim is made in this increment.
