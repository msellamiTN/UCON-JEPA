## 1. Experimental scope

Five predeclared generator seeds were executed:

`20261120, 20261121, 20261122, 20261123, 20261124`.

Each seed generates 4,500 episodes and 36,000 transitions (five synthetic behavior policies × three policy families × 300 episodes × eight transitions). Across five seeds this is 22,500 episodes and 180,000 transitions.

For every seed:
- in-policy train/validation/calibration/test evaluation was executed;
- all five leave-one-policy-out folds were executed;
- each held-out policy was excluded from train, validation and calibration;
- checkpoint selection used validation and temperature selection used calibration;
- paired episode-cluster bootstrap intervals used 1,000 replicates and resampled whole episodes, not transitions.

The paired differences are defined as **past-only softmax minus baseline**. Negative values favor softmax for NLL/Brier; positive values favor softmax for top-1/top-2 accuracy. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on each generated test set; between-seed standard deviation is reported separately.

For each fold and seed, the artifact retains 1,000-replicate paired whole-episode bootstrap intervals against all three baselines. The intervals show the same broad policy heterogeneity: softmax is substantially worse than historical frequency on goal-directed and retrying-adversary holdouts, much worse on uniform NLL/Brier, and better on the planner/explorer holdouts. The LOPO folds share overlapping training populations and must not be treated as independent policy samples.

## 4. Scientific verdict

- **In-policy H1:** supported for this declared synthetic generator and five seeds on NLL, top-1 and Brier against the listed baselines; not supported on top-2 versus Markov.
- **Policy generalization H5:** not supported. The softmax model fails to generalize consistently across held-out synthetic behavior policies, and the historical-frequency baseline wins the LOPO macro comparison.
- **Calibration:** still needs policy-shift scrutiny; aggregate ECE alone does not establish reliable probabilities on every held-out policy.
- **E2 predicted-action JEPA, E3 joint JEPA, E4 intervention utility:** not evaluated here.
- **Real agent/LLM policy generalization:** not evaluated.

The low across-seed standard deviations indicate that these five seeds are stable under this particular generator; they do not establish external validity. The bootstrap intervals are conditional on synthetic test episodes and are not population intervals for real agents. No multiplicity correction is applied to the exploratory fold/metric comparisons.

## 5. Decision and next action

**G8.4.8.3.1 implementation, five-seed execution, paired episode-bootstrap analysis and CI passed.** The key scientific conclusion is not that the supervised action model generalizes, but that it is strong in-policy and weak under several policy shifts.

Do not move directly to an unqualified JEPA claim. Next, preserve the action uncertainty explicitly in E2, compare predicted-action forecasting with the privileged known-action diagnostic, and carry the historical-frequency/Markov baselines forward.
