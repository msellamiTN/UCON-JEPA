## 1. Experimental protocol

- Seeds: `20261120, 20261121, 20261122`.
- Five held-out policies, one per LOPO fold: `goal_directed`, `uniform`, `retrying_adversary`, `prerequisite_blind_planner`, `denial_adaptive_explorer`.
- In each fold the held-out policy is absent from train, validation and calibration; test contains only that policy.
- Horizons: 1, 2, 4 and 8 transitions.
- E2 uses 32 Monte Carlo action-sequence samples per window.
- Paired comparisons use 1,000 whole-episode bootstrap replicates per seed and fold.
- Complete G8.4 regression suite passed before the benchmark.

Comparators are the post-hoc JEPA-latent action head and the joint action/state JEPA with auxiliary action loss. A raw-context action predictor is retained as a one-step diagnostic only; it is not used for recursive E2 rollouts.

E1 receives the realized future action sequence and is a privileged, non-deployable diagnostic. E2 receives only the initial context and samples its own autoregressive future actions. Neither future actions, future observations nor future security labels are E2 inference inputs.

Key findings:

1. **Goal-directed:** joint model improves Brier at horizons 1/2/4, with all three seed-specific paired episode-bootstrap intervals below zero. At h=8 it reverses sharply; all three intervals favor the post-hoc model.
2. **Uniform:** joint model is slightly worse at h=1/2, slightly better at h=4/8. Evidence is mixed across seed-specific intervals.
3. **Retrying adversary:** joint model improves Brier at h=1/2/4, but seed-level paired intervals are mixed; h=8 is effectively tied.
4. **Prerequisite-blind planner:** small h=1 improvement; joint model is worse at h=2/4/8. At h=4 all three seed-specific intervals favor the post-hoc model.
5. **Denial-adaptive explorer:** joint model is slightly worse at h=1/2/4 and essentially tied at h=8.

AUROC is undefined in policy/horizon strata without both positive and negative labels; it is therefore not filled with artificial values. At h=8, the joint model's AUROC is low for the strata where it is defined: uniform 0.447, prerequisite-blind planner 0.392 and denial-adaptive explorer 0.382. This is not dependable long-horizon discrimination.

## 5. Statistical interpretation and limitations

- Paired whole-episode bootstrap intervals are computed within each seed and held-out policy. They are descriptive conditional on the synthetic generator and sampled episodes.
- No multiplicity correction is applied across the 5 policies × 4 horizons × 2 outcomes. Do not read isolated intervals as family-wise confirmatory evidence.
- The five LOPO folds share overlapping training policies; they are not five independent samples from a real population of agents.
- Three random seeds are not enough to establish robustness across data-generating processes.
- These are five hand-designed synthetic policies, not empirically sampled deployed agents.
- Monte Carlo spread reflects sampled action-sequence variability; it does not quantify model epistemic uncertainty.
- E1 is privileged and non-deployable. E2 remains materially harder.
- No real-agent generalization, operational intervention utility, or production security assurance has been established.

## 6. Verdict and next gate

**G8.4.8.6 passes as an implementation and experiment. The scientific claim of policy-robust joint JEPA anticipation is not supported.**

The auxiliary action objective helps on selected policy/horizon combinations, but it is not a consistent solution to behavior-policy shift. At horizon 8, discrimination is poor and the equal-policy macro metrics favor the post-hoc model. We should not tune against these test-fold outcomes and then report the same folds as independent evidence.

The next research step is not simply another larger model. First establish a stronger policy-shift protocol with more diverse behavior generators and controlled shifts in policy parameters, then add calibration and uncertainty diagnostics on separate validation/calibration populations. Consider a policy-agnostic action prior or explicit mixture over policy hypotheses, but compare it against historical-frequency and Markov baselines under the same held-out-policy boundary. Pre-register the experiment before looking at the new test outcomes.

Formal UCON remains the only authorization authority. JEPA outputs remain advisory; no learned prediction can authorize an operation or override a formal DENY.
